INCLUZIV PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE BASE - CONVERSATIONAL Q&A FOR VOICE AGENT TRAINING
================================================================================

This document is a comprehensive conversational knowledge base covering the
Incluziv ERP, Incluziv Cloud (Neo), and Incluziv Retail product suite for home
furnishings and textile distribution businesses. It is organized as natural
User / AI Agent conversations, grouped by topic, intended to train an AI voice
agent to speak with customers and users about the product in a natural,
helpful way.

================================================================================

TOPIC: GETTING STARTED, LOGIN & SECURITY

Conversation 1
User: Hi, how do I log in to Incluziv, and is it secure?
AI Agent: Sure. When you go to your Incluziv login page, you'll first see a captcha. That's there to stop bots or automated programs from trying to break into your account. After the captcha, you enter your username and password.
User: Is that it, or is there anything else?
AI Agent: There's one more step. After your password, you're asked for an OTP, which is a one-time password. It's sent to your registered email, and if you've set it up, it can also come to your WhatsApp number. Once you enter that OTP, you're logged in.
User: Can I choose whether it comes by email or WhatsApp?
AI Agent: Yes, you can configure the OTP to go to either your email or your WhatsApp number, depending on what's set up for your account.

---
Conversation 2
User: Why does Incluziv keep asking me to change my password?
AI Agent: That's the 30-day password policy. For security, your password is set to expire every 30 days.
User: Why 30 days specifically?
AI Agent: It's mainly to check that the account is actually being used actively. If a password hasn't been changed in 30 days, or the account hasn't been accessed in a while, the system prompts you to reset it.
User: Will I get a warning before it actually expires?
AI Agent: Yes. You'll start seeing an alert on your dashboard about seven days before your password is due to expire, so you're not caught off guard.
User: What happens if I ignore the warning and it expires anyway?
AI Agent: When you try to log in after your password has expired, you'll see a message saying your password has expired. From that same screen, you can click reset password, confirm your old password, and set a new one. You can even reuse your old password if you want to.

---
Conversation 3
User: I forgot my password. What do I do?
AI Agent: No problem. On the login page, click on "Forgot Password."
User: Then what?
AI Agent: You'll be asked to enter your username. After that, you'll usually get a choice of where to receive your OTP, like your registered email ID or mobile number, depending on what's been set up for security.
User: And after I get the OTP?
AI Agent: Enter the OTP, and you'll be able to set a new password right there. Once that's done, you can log in with your new password.
User: What if I don't remember my username either?
AI Agent: You can still use the same forgot password flow with your registered email ID to receive an OTP, verify yourself, and set a new username and password from there.

---
Conversation 4
User: One of my team members says their account is locked and they can't remember their password. Can I help them as an admin?
AI Agent: Yes, if you're an admin user, you can reset passwords for your team directly.
User: How do I do that?
AI Agent: Go to Utility, and from there open Reset Password. Search for the user by their username, select them, and reset their password.
User: What happens after I reset it?
AI Agent: Once you reset a user's password, their login name automatically becomes their new password. They should log in with that and then change it to something of their own on first login.
User: Is this the same process in Incluziv ERP and Incluziv Cloud?
AI Agent: Yes, this works the same way in both Incluziv ERP and Incluziv Cloud. You'll find Reset Password under the Utility menu in either one.

---
Conversation 3b
User: How do I recover my password in Incluziv Retail?
AI Agent: It's quite simple. On the login page, click "Forgot Password," then enter your username.
User: Then?
AI Agent: Select your OTP option, and you'll receive an OTP by email. Copy that OTP into the application and set your new password.
User: Can I just change my password normally without forgetting it?
AI Agent: Yes. You can click on "Change Password," enter your old password along with your new password, confirm it, and your password is reset.

Conversation 5
User: How do I create a new user in Incluziv?
AI Agent: Sure. Go to the Utility menu, and from there open User Master. There's an "Add User" button you can use to create a new one.
User: What details do I need to fill in?
AI Agent: You'll need the display name, the user's role, their login name, password, and email ID. You can also add an alternate mobile number, birthday, and anniversary, though those last few are optional.
User: Why does the email ID matter so much?
AI Agent: Your email ID and mobile number are used whenever OTPs or password reset emails are sent, so it's important to enter the correct ones for that user.
User: What about giving them permission to add or edit records?
AI Agent: That's set per user too. If you mark "Add New" as yes for a menu, they can create new records there. If it's no, they can't, even if they can see that section. The same applies separately to editing and deleting rights.
User: Anything else I need to set up when creating a user?
AI Agent: Yes, one important thing: every user needs to be linked to at least one warehouse when you create them. If your account has just one warehouse, they're automatically tied to it. If you have more than one, you choose whether the user can access all warehouses or only specific ones.

Conversation 6b

User: That's a bit confusing. Can you give an example?
AI Agent: Sure. Say you want to give someone access to Sales Order related menus. If you don't enable the very first menu under that group, none of the other sales-related permissions you gave them will actually show up, even though you assigned them.

AI Agent: Sure, I can walk you through that. Would you like me to explain the steps?

User: How does the salesperson actually log into the mobile app?
AI Agent: For that, you create a separate sales rep login through Agent Sales Representative Login, where you select the customer representative, set a display name and username, and save it. That username and password is what you give your salesperson to log into the mobile app after downloading it from the Play Store or App Store.
User: Is there anything else they need to log in on the app?
AI Agent: Yes, they'll also need the CRN number, which is available in the software, along with an OTP to authorize their first login.

AI Agent: Yes, the same steps apply whether you're setting this up in Incluziv ERP or Incluziv Cloud.

TOPIC: COMPANY & WAREHOUSE MASTER SETUP

Conversation 1
User: Hi, where do I update my company details in Incluziv?
AI Agent: Sure. Go to Settings, and under the Organization tab you'll find Company Master. Since this is usually pre-filled when your account is set up, you'd search for your company and use the modify option to make changes.
User: What kind of details can I update there?
AI Agent: You can update your company name, PAN number, CIN number if you're a private limited company, website, and other basic details. You can also update your bank details and your contact person.
User: Why does it matter if this information is accurate?
AI Agent: Because these details get printed at the footer of your invoices, delivery notes, and other documents. So if you ever notice the footer address isn't correct, that's the place to fix it.
User: What about the bank details in the Company Master specifically?
AI Agent: Those are relevant if you're using the same bank account across all your warehouses and your entire company. If different warehouses use different bank accounts, that's handled separately in the Warehouse Master instead.

---
Conversation 2
User: How is the Warehouse Master different from the Company Master?
AI Agent: The Warehouse Master holds details specific to each warehouse, like its city, state, and PIN code, along with information like parcel sticker details, GST number, and bank details for that warehouse.
User: Why is the PIN code so important there?
AI Agent: Whenever you generate an e-invoice or e-way bill, the system calculates the distance between your warehouse and the customer's shipping address, and it uses the PIN codes from both the Warehouse Master and the customer's shipping address to do that. So getting the PIN code right really matters.
User: What's this parcel sticker information field for?
AI Agent: That's the name and details that get printed on your parcel stickers, at the bottom of the sticker.
User: Can I also print a UPI QR code on my invoices?
AI Agent: Yes, there's a UPI ID field in the Warehouse Master. Once you add your UPI ID there, it prints a QR code on your invoices, quotations, and even delivery notes.
User: What if I run two companies out of the same warehouse?
AI Agent: You can handle that too. You can link multiple companies to the same warehouse, and configure separate bank details and GST numbers for each company at the warehouse level. That way, the correct bank details and GST number print on the documents for each company.

TOPIC: INCLUZIV RETAIL - SETTING UP VENDORS AND ITEMS

Conversation 6
User: How do I add a new vendor or supplier in Incluziv Retail?
AI Agent: You can go to Vendor Master and click New Vendor. You'll enter the vendor name, an optional vendor code, credit period, and other details like PAN number, which aren't compulsory.
User: What if a supplier has more than one billing name?
AI Agent: That's supported. You can create a single supplier record with multiple billing names, so whenever they supply material, you can book their purchase invoices under the correct billing name.
User: Is there a faster way to add many vendors at once instead of one by one?
AI Agent: Yes, you can upload vendors in bulk using Excel. There's a sample upload sheet you can download, which shows you the mandatory and non-mandatory fields.
User: Anything I should be careful about when using the bulk upload sheet?
AI Agent: Yes, when filling it in, make sure you enter the billing name at the end. Also keep in mind that through the Excel upload, you can only set one billing name per vendor. If a vendor has more than one billing name, you'll need to add the additional one manually in the Vendor Master afterward.
User: What happens after I choose the file and upload it?
AI Agent: The system shows you all the details from the file so you can verify them before saving. Once you save, all the vendors get created. If you didn't specify a vendor code, the system assigns a default code automatically.

---
Conversation 7
User: Once my vendors are set up, how do I add their items?
AI Agent: You can add items manually by clicking New Item, then selecting the vendor and the product category.
User: What details go into an item?
AI Agent: Things like the collection or brand name, HSN code, roll price, cut price, and MRP. The MRP is the price you intend to sell at, while roll price and cut price are your wholesale and retail purchase prices for the fabric.
User: Can one item have multiple serial numbers at different prices?
AI Agent: Yes, you can add multiple serial numbers under the same collection, and each serial number can carry its own price if needed, or share the same price as the rest.
User: Is there a bulk way to add items instead of doing it one at a time?
AI Agent: Yes, under Items you'll find Import Items. You select the vendor and product category, then use the sample file to fill in fields like retailer collection name, distributor collection name, serial number, HSN, roll price, cut price, and MRP.
User: Can I give a collection a different name for my own store than what the distributor calls it?
AI Agent: Yes. If you don't want to show the supplier's collection name to your retail customers, you can set your own retailer collection name in that field, similar to how some businesses paste their own sticker over the supplier's label.
User: What happens if I upload the same file again later just to update prices?
AI Agent: As long as the retailer collection name, serial number, and vendor stay the same, the system will simply update, or overwrite, the price on the existing item rather than creating a duplicate.
User: And if I accidentally change the collection name or vendor when re-uploading?
AI Agent: In that case, the system creates a brand new duplicate item instead of updating the existing one, since it treats it as a different item. So it's important to keep the collection name and vendor consistent if your intention is just to update prices.

TOPIC: TAX MASTER SETUP

Conversation 8
User: How do I set up a new tax rate in Incluziv Cloud?
AI Agent: Sure. Go to Masters and open GST Tax Master. Whenever you need to set up a new tax, click Add New.
User: What do I need to fill in?
AI Agent: You'll enter the GST percentage, and the system splits it into CGST and SGST for domestic transactions, along with an IGST rate for interstate transactions. Once you save it, that tax is ready to use in your purchase operations.
User: Is it the same process in Incluziv ERP?
AI Agent: It's a bit different there. Would you like me to explain?
User: Yes, please.
AI Agent: In Incluziv ERP, setting up a new tax is a two-step process. First, you create the tax itself in the Operations module, under Masters, in the GST Tax Master, the same way, by clicking Add New and entering your CGST, SGST, and IGST percentages.
User: What's the second step?
AI Agent: After creating the tax in Operations, you need to go into the Finance module and assign a ledger for that tax. If the ledger already exists, that's fine, but if not, you create it under Ledger Master, typically under Duties and Taxes, for each of CGST, SGST, and IGST.
User: What happens if I skip that ledger assignment step?
AI Agent: You won't be able to use that new tax in your purchase transactions until the ledger is assigned. So it's important not to skip that finance-side step after creating the tax in Operations.

TOPIC: CREATING ITEMS AND CUSTOMERS IN BULK USING EXCEL

Conversation 1
User: I'm new to Incluziv Cloud. What's the best way to load all my items and customers into the system at once?
AI Agent: Good question. Since item and customer data are the most critical data for a new setup, there's a bulk upload option built for exactly this. Would you like me to walk you through it?
User: Yes, please.
AI Agent: Go to Utility, then Upload Link. You'll see two options there: Upload Items and Upload Bulk Customer. Each one has an Instructions tab where you can download a demo Excel file to fill in.
User: What fields does the item file need?
AI Agent: It includes collection, serial number, quality, design, shade, item name, RRP, vendor name, purchase price, reorder lead time, HSN code, color tag, and brand.
User: Some of those terms are new to me, like collection and serial number.
AI Agent: If you work with fabrics, wallpapers, or laminates, you're probably used to catalog books. The collection is basically the name of that catalog book, and the serial number is the number printed on the label behind each fabric sample in that book.
User: What about quality, design, and shade?
AI Agent: Those are optional and mostly used in the fabric business. Quality might describe the base material, design describes the pattern, and shade describes the color. Together with quality, design, and shade, you build a unique item name for each fabric.
User: What if I don't use quality, design, and shade at all?
AI Agent: That's fine. You can go to Product Category Master, find your fabric category, and set QDS as no. Once you do that, you won't be required to fill those fields at all.
User: And what about the customer file?
AI Agent: That one has fields like serial number, customer name, PAN number, credit limit, payment terms, broker, brokerage, address line one and two, country, state, city, pin code, GSTIN, contact person, designation, contact number, email ID, and a courier.
User: Anything I need to be careful about there?
AI Agent: A couple of things. Your address lines need at least three characters each, since that's required for e-invoicing. The pin code is also mandatory for the same reason. And if a customer doesn't have a GSTIN, you enter a dash to mark them as URP, meaning an unregistered party. If Excel tries to treat that dash as a formula, just put a single quote before it so it's treated as plain text.
User: Do I have to use Excel, or can I add these one at a time too?
AI Agent: You can absolutely add items and customers one by one using the Item Master and Customer Master forms. The Excel upload is just there to save time when you need to create a large number of them at once.

TOPIC: ITEM MASTER SETUP IN INCLUZIV CLOUD

Conversation 2
User: How do I manually create a new item in Incluziv Cloud?
AI Agent: Go to Masters, then Items, and click Add New. First, you'll choose the product category, like Fabrics.
User: What happens after I choose Fabrics?
AI Agent: If that category has quality, design, and shade enabled, those three fields will open up for you to fill in. You can select existing values, or click the plus sign to add a new quality, design, or shade on the spot.
User: Does the item name get created automatically?
AI Agent: Yes, it's automatically built as a combination of quality, design, and shade, but you can edit it if you'd rather call it something simpler.
User: What's this "standard piece length" field about?
AI Agent: That defines the length above which an order is treated as a roll order. So if you set it to 60 meters, any order of 60 meters or more would be fulfilled as a roll.
User: What if I don't want a particular item to show up in my reorder report?
AI Agent: There's a field called "Exclude from Reordering." If you set that to yes, the item won't be picked up when the reorder report runs.
User: Can I set the vendor and pricing at the same time as creating the item?
AI Agent: Yes, on the same screen you can link a vendor, give a different vendor-specific product name if needed, set the purchase price, MOQ, and reorder lead time, and even add the item to price lists, discount lists, or a collection, all in one go.
User: Is there a bulk upload option here too?
AI Agent: Yes, the same Upload Items feature under Utility works here, using a sample file with the same fields you'd fill in manually.
User: Once my items are created, how do I load my opening stock?
AI Agent: You can go to Upload Opening Stock, either using Excel, or using a smart option where you select the warehouse, then pick the collection and serial number, and just fill in the quantity and reference piece number. You can also add stock manually, one piece at a time, specifying the piece number, quantity, purchase price, and whether it's a roll or a cut piece.

---
Conversation 3
User: What do I need to set up before I even start creating items, like in Product Category Master?
AI Agent: When you create a new product category, you'll define its name, its unit of measurement, and a few key settings.
User: What settings matter most?
AI Agent: Whether decimals are allowed, for example fabric can be sold as 5.5 meters, but bedsheets usually can't be sold in decimals. Whether piece-wise tracking is on, which gives every physical piece a unique piece number. Whether brokerage sale is enabled, if you pay brokers on sales in that category. And whether QDS, meaning quality, design, and shade, is available for that category.
User: What if I turn QDS off for a category?
AI Agent: Then you simply don't need to fill in quality, design, or shade for any item in that category. It becomes a much simpler item entry.

TOPIC: CREATING ITEMS IN INCLUZIV ERP

Conversation 4
User: How is creating items different in Incluziv ERP compared to Incluziv Cloud?
AI Agent: The core idea is similar. You'd start in Settings, at Product Category Master, where categories like Fabrics or catalogs are usually already set up. You can add a new one using the New button if needed.
User: What if I have the same fabric appearing in two different catalogs?
AI Agent: Good question. In that case, you keep the item name, and the quality, design, and shade, exactly the same across both catalogs, but you can change the collection name and serial number for each one. That way, the system treats it as one item with a main collection and a linked sub-collection, instead of creating duplicate stock.
User: What if my vendor name doesn't match while uploading the Excel file?
AI Agent: The vendor needs to already exist in the Vendor Master before you upload items. If the vendor name in your Excel file doesn't exactly match the name in the system, the vendor won't get linked to that item.
User: What if the system throws an error about an HSN code?
AI Agent: That usually means the HSN code you used isn't available yet. Go to Settings, then HSN Code Master under Organization, and check if it exists. If not, you can add the HSN code there and select the applicable tax for it.
User: Can I create items for more than one product category or company at the same time?
AI Agent: Not in a single upload. You can only create items for one product category and one company at a time when uploading in bulk.

User: How do I set that up?

User: What happens after I upload it back?

TOPIC: ASSIGNING LEDGERS FOR CUSTOMERS AND ITEMS

Conversation 7
User: I'm trying to create an invoice, but I got a message saying customer ledger details are not present in master data. What does that mean?
AI Agent: That means the customer's ledger hasn't been linked yet in the Finance module. I can walk you through fixing it.
User: Please do.
AI Agent: Go to the Finance module and open Assign Ledger. Under Party Ledger, you'll find Customer Ledger. Search for the customer showing the issue, and you'll see them listed as one whose ledger isn't assigned yet. Select them and add the ledger.
User: Once I do that, can I generate the invoice right away?
AI Agent: Yes, once the customer's ledger is assigned, the bill is ready to be generated and printed.
User: You mentioned a group earlier. What's that about?
AI Agent: When you're assigning ledgers, you'll notice a parent group is involved, for example a sales group. That comes from Group Master, where each group has an entity type, like customer or vendor, and that determines where it's available for linking.
User: Does the same kind of issue happen for items?
AI Agent: Yes. If an item's ledger isn't assigned, you'll see a similar message when billing. You'd go to the same Assign Ledger screen, but this time look at the item ledger option, where the relevant ledgers, like fabric sales, would already be set up under the parent sales group for you to assign.
User: Does this apply to purchases too?
AI Agent: Yes, when you're booking a purchase, you'd similarly need the vendor ledger assigned, and for export customers, you'd need the export customer ledger assigned before you can invoice them.

TOPIC: CUSTOMER MASTER SETUP

Conversation 8
User: How do I create a new customer manually in Incluziv Cloud?
AI Agent: Go to Masters, then Customer Master, and click Add New. You'll fill in basic details, and you can set a credit limit for the customer.
User: What if I don't want any credit limit at all for a customer?
AI Agent: You can set the credit limit to minus one, and that gives them unlimited credit. If you do want a limit, the system tracks their outstanding balance plus their orders in progress against that limit and alerts you once it's crossed.
User: What other links do I need to set for a customer?

TOPIC: COMPLETING THE FINANCIAL YEAR END PROCESS

Conversation 1
User: Our financial year is ending soon. How do we actually close it out in Incluziv ERP?
AI Agent: When you log in, a pop-up will appear on your dashboard with short information about the financial year end activity. Before you start, there are a couple of things to check first.
User: What should I check before starting?
AI Agent: The pop-up mentions an email ID, and you need to make sure that email is active and accessible, since all the OTPs for this activity get sent there. If there's an issue with that email, you'd contact the Incluziv team to get it sorted. Also, only a user with an admin role can complete this activity.
User: Anything about my browser settings I need to worry about?
AI Agent: Yes, pop-ups need to be enabled in your browser, since they're required later for downloading reports. In Chrome, you'd click the three dots, go to Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Site Settings, then Pop-ups and Redirects, and allow pop-ups. If you're using a different browser, you can look up how to enable pop-ups there.
User: Once that's set up, how do I actually start the process?
AI Agent: There's a Request button at the bottom of the pop-up. Click that to raise your request.
User: Does clicking Request immediately stop me from creating new transactions?
AI Agent: No, you can still continue creating transaction documents even after clicking Request. Our suggestion is to raise the request first and then continue with your regular work in the meantime.
User: What happens after I click Request?
AI Agent: An OTP gets sent to your registered email address. Copy it, paste it into the text box, and click Verify and Continue. Your request then goes to the Incluziv team, and once it's approved, you'll get a confirmation notification.
User: Where do I go once the request is approved?
AI Agent: You'll see an announcement pop up on your dashboard, and there's a "Start Financial Year Process" menu. You can also find the same activity under Utility, in FY Activity. Either option gets you started.
User: What's the first thing I need to do once I start the process?
AI Agent: You'll select your company. If you have multiple companies set up, you'll need to complete this activity separately for each one, one at a time.
User: What comes after selecting the company?
AI Agent: You'll see your document series setup, covering things like your invoice, credit note, and debit note series, and general invoice series if that's enabled for you. You just need to check and confirm that everything's correct, since this cannot be modified after activation.
User: What if I spot an issue with one of these series?
AI Agent: You can raise a support ticket or reach out to the Incluziv help desk if there's a problem with any of your series before confirming.
User: What happens after I confirm the series?
AI Agent: You'll tick a mandatory declaration checkbox and click Confirm and Proceed. After that, you'll see a set of mandatory reports, like the stock report, customer aging summary, customer aging detail, accounts receivable report, and quarantine report, which you need to download.
User: Is that download step quick?
AI Agent: It can take a little time depending on your data, so just wait for it to complete. Once downloaded, a statutory reports section opens up, for example with a GSTR-1 report. If you've already downloaded what you need, or don't want to right now, you can click Skip and Proceed instead.
User: What's the final step?
AI Agent: You'll reach a Finish option. Read the instructions on that screen carefully, since there's a Restart button available there too, in case something goes wrong during the process and you need to start over. Click Finish, and you'll get one more OTP to verify. Paste it in and click Continue.
User: What happens once I submit that final OTP?
AI Agent: The final process begins, running some mandatory back-end updates, which can take a few minutes. You might see some technical-looking messages on screen during this, but you can safely ignore those and just wait for it to complete. Once it's done, you'll see a success message confirming the process passed.
User: Does the Restart option still work at that point if something goes wrong?
AI Agent: No, once you've submitted that final OTP, the Restart option no longer works. It's only available for use before that final OTP verification step, for example if your system shuts down or you lose internet connectivity partway through.
User: What happens to our ability to create invoices and other documents while all this is going on?
AI Agent: If you're doing this activity on the very last day of the financial year, once it's complete, you won't be able to create new transaction documents until the first day of the next financial year. Separately, if the financial year has already ended and you haven't run this activity yet, you also won't be able to create any transaction documents until you complete it.
User: So what would you recommend if we're doing this right on the last day of the year?
AI Agent: We'd recommend completing all your pending transactions first, and only then running the financial year end activity, so you're not caught needing to create a document partway through the process.
User: Is there anything I should check once the new financial year starts and I create my first document?
AI Agent: Yes, when you create your first transaction document in the new year, it's worth verifying the document series once to make sure everything picked up correctly. If you run into any issues at that point, you can reach out for support.

AI Agent: A Child Piece uses whichever collection it was created under, whether that's a Primary Collection or a Sub Collection. A Master Piece, on the other hand, always uses the Primary Collection.

AI Agent: If your FOC limit is set to 0.75, any leftover quantity smaller than 0.75 automatically gets treated as Free of Cost. AI Agent: It's only available during the Cutting Sheet process. It doesn't apply to any other process in the system.
User: Can I switch Piece-wise Stocking on or off whenever I want?
